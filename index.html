States

Normal is not the only state.

Every production component will define loading, empty, error, success/result and unavailable behavior where relevant.

Loading

Subtle, non-blocking feedback.

Empty

Helpful next action, not a dead end.

Error

Explain what happened and how to recover.

Success

Confirm the result and next step.
